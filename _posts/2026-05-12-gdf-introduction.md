Welcome to Game Dev Fundamentals Series. The aim of this series is adopting engine-agnostic game development with covering topics such as gameplay mechanics and systems, AI and user interface.

Although modern game engines are very popular and efficient, I think it's still important to lean towards some old school game development. This doesn't mean writing things entirely from scratch such as rendering and physics, but being able to adapt industry standards independent from specific implementations of game engines. This is important, because otherwise you're in risk being vulnerable to external factors affecting your development cycle negatively. A well known example is Unity's 2023 Runtime Fee controversy.
